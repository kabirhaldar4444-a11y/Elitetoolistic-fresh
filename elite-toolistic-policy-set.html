Charter II Privacy Policy

Information Stewardship Instrument

The Academy's framework for candidate records, identity verification and data security.

IN BRIEF Charter II Summary

Candidate information supports admission, identity assurance, curriculum access, evaluation, invoicing and credential records. It is not sold for unrelated marketing.

01

Custodian of the record

ELITE TOOLISTIC (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED is responsible for personal information processed across admission, checkout, verification, learning, evaluation, certification and candidate support.

Privacy correspondence may be delivered through the official inquiry channel or to the registered seat at 1444, Gaur City 1, Sector 4, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201318.

02

Candidate-supplied information

  • Legal name, contact details, address and account credentials.
  • Billing entity, transaction details and tax identifiers.
  • Government photo identification or another approved identity record.
  • A short video verification and resulting identity status.
  • Support communications and evidence voluntarily submitted.
03

Academic and technical records

  • Enrolment, curriculum access, submission, score and certificate history.
  • Invoice, payment-reference, credit and refund records.
  • IP address, device, browser, login, stream and file-release events.
  • Error, integrity and suspected-fraud logs.

Full payment credentials are normally entered directly with the payment processor rather than retained by the Academy.

04

Purposes and legal grounds

Information is used to establish candidature, verify identity, fulfil the purchased curriculum, preserve examination integrity, create correct invoices and credentials, provide support, secure the platform and meet accounting or legal obligations.

The applicable legal ground may be performance of the candidate agreement, consent for particular sensitive processing, a statutory duty or a permitted legitimate institutional interest.

05

Document and video verification

Identity and video records receive heightened safeguards. They are used to prevent impersonation, connect an evaluation to the correct candidate and maintain a reliable credential record.

Required disclosure before collection

The production notice must name the verification processor, say whether biometric geometry or a template is created, identify the processing location and publish a precise retention schedule.

06

Authorised recipients

Limited data may be processed by contracted payment, cloud, identity, communications, analytics, security and professional advisers. A financial institution may receive relevant order and fulfilment evidence during a transaction dispute. Courts and public authorities receive information only under a lawful requirement.

The Academy does not sell or rent candidate identity or biometric information to unrelated commercial lists.

07

Retention and institutional security

Records are held only for documented academic, credential-verification, tax, fraud-prevention or legal needs. Raw identification and video files should be removed or irreversibly de-identified when their verified purpose and mandatory period end.

Security measures include encrypted transport, restricted roles, audit logs and processor oversight. Candidates should use unique passwords and report suspected compromise immediately.

08

Candidate rights

Depending on applicable law, a candidate may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability; object to specified processing; withdraw consent; or complain to the competent authority. Identity is verified before a request is fulfilled, and records may remain where law or defensible fraud-prevention requires them.

Charter I Terms & Conditions

Code of Candidate Engagement

The contractual standards for enrolment, scholarly access, evaluation and private certification.

IN BRIEF Charter I Summary

A candidate receives personal access to the curriculum described at enrolment. Self-paced study is the default; counsel or live review exists only when the chosen program expressly grants it.

01

Constitution of the agreement

This Code forms the agreement between the candidate and ELITE TOOLISTIC (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("the Academy"). It is accepted through registration, payment, an acceptance control or first use of protected academic material.

The published curriculum, fee, duration and stated inclusions for the selected program form part of this agreement.

02

Institutional character

The Academy provides independent vocational, administrative and executive education through structured digital curricula. Alignment with an industry framework or international practice does not by itself mean recognition, accreditation or licensing by a university, government authority or professional regulator.

Unless a course expressly identifies an external awarding body, every certificate is an internal private credential reflecting the Academy's own assessment standards.

03

Mode of study and scholarly counsel

The ordinary mode is self-paced study through recorded lectures, written material and digital evaluation. A designated counselor, live advisory session or direct review is included only where the course description or order confirmation states it.

The candidate is responsible for reading the curriculum description, maintaining suitable equipment and completing each requirement within the allotted period.

04

Candidate identity and account integrity

Accurate legal, billing and contact particulars are required. The Academy may require a government photo ID and brief video verification to connect the account, evaluator and certificate to the same candidate.

Accounts are individual and non-transferable. False records, proxy examinations, shared access or deliberate evasion of verification may result in a security hold or termination.

05

Custody of academic works

Lectures, papers, templates, assessments, archives, software and marks remain the property of the Academy or its licensors. The candidate receives a limited, personal licence for study during the designated period.

Prohibited acts

Redistribution, resale, stream capture, automated extraction, publication of examination questions, credential sharing and circumvention of access controls are forbidden.

Authorised files delivered through expiring or single-use links should be preserved promptly for personal study.

06

Evaluation and private credentials

Examination access follows the period and conditions stated for the curriculum. The registered candidate must complete the evaluation personally. Results may be produced by electronic grading rules and reviewed where the program provides for it.

An enrolment document records current candidature. A provisional, completion or merit credential records the relevant internal result. No award promises appointment, remuneration, promotion or acceptance by a third party.

07

Fees, taxation and academic revision

The Academy states the fee and applicable tax before payment. A GST or other required invoice is placed in the candidate's record after payment and validation.

The Refund Policy governs cancellation after digital delivery. The Academy may refine sequence, examples, reading lists or evaluation tools to preserve relevance and security, without materially diminishing the purchased curriculum.

08

Discipline, liability and jurisdiction

The Academy may suspend access while investigating payment fraud, identity conflict, malicious code, unlawful copying or shared accounts and may terminate for a material breach. A review route will be provided where reasonably appropriate.

Service is delivered with reasonable care, but uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed. These terms are governed by the laws of India, subject to mandatory rights and [confirm competent court jurisdiction]. Notices may be submitted through the official contact page or to the registered seat.

Charter IV Refund Policy

Financial Review & Remedy Code

The Academy's rules for final digital supply, correction of errors and lawful remedies.

IN BRIEF Charter IV Summary

Digital curriculum fees are ordinarily final after protected material is released. Duplicate charges, material non-delivery and mandatory rights remain open to formal review.

01

Finality of released curricula

Digital lectures and texts can remain with a candidate after access. Once immediate performance is requested and protected material is supplied, a change-of-mind cancellation is ordinarily unavailable, subject to any consumer remedy that the law does not permit the Academy to exclude.

02

Grounds that ordinarily do not qualify

  • A later preference for classroom or personalised instruction not listed in the curriculum.
  • Failure to complete verification, study or evaluation within the allotted period.
  • Dissatisfaction with a score, employment result or external recognition.
  • Problems caused by personal hardware, software, storage, firewall or connectivity.
  • Loss of an authorised file after successful release.
  • Termination for fraud, impersonation, credential sharing, scraping or redistribution.
03

Matters admitted for review

The Academy reviews duplicate payment, payment without admission creation, substantial departure from the enrolment description, persistent Academy-side non-delivery, incorrect billing and remedies made compulsory by applicable law.

Available resolutions may include correction, restoration or extension of access, repeated fulfilment, replacement, credit, or a proportionate partial or full refund.

04

Immediate digital performance

Where required, enrolment should separately record the candidate's request for digital supply to begin immediately and acknowledgement of its effect on a statutory cancellation period. The accepted text, order and time should be preserved in the register.

05

Petition for review

Submit a request through the official contact registry within [confirm petition period]. Include the order reference, candidate email, payment date and clear evidence. Passwords, complete card numbers and unredacted identity records must not be sent through ordinary correspondence.

06

Determination and banking disputes

The Academy verifies the applicant, examines payment and access records, attempts a service correction where suitable and communicates a decision within [confirm determination period]. Approved funds return by the original payment route.

A genuine statutory chargeback right is not restricted. If a financial institution investigates, the Academy may supply relevant enrolment acceptance, verification and content-release records. Deliberate misrepresentation may prompt a fraud inquiry.

Charter III Service Delivery

Academic Fulfilment Protocol

The documented sequence from cleared enrolment to final evaluation and credential record.

IN BRIEF Charter III Summary

The fulfilment register records nine stages: admission, identity assurance, invoicing, curriculum release, candidature, lectures, examination and final result.

01

Standard method of instruction

The Academy's standard curriculum is self-paced and delivered through recorded lectures, written study archives and digital evaluation. A counselor, live seminar or direct review is provided only when the program record expressly includes it.

02

The nine-stage register

  1. Admission is enteredCleared payment creates the candidate record, access route and welcome notice.
  2. Document identity is examinedThe candidate submits an accepted government photo ID.
  3. Video identity is confirmedA brief recorded check helps prevent substitution and proxy testing.
  4. The fiscal record is issuedThe applicable GST or tax invoice is normally posted within 24 hours after validation.
  5. The written archive is openedPDF texts, frameworks and workbooks are released for personal study.
  6. Candidature is documentedAn enrolment record confirms active participation where applicable.
  7. Recorded instruction is enabledPersonal access to the lecture collection and included support elements begins.
  8. Evaluation credentials are grantedExamination access follows the program duration and published conditions.
  9. The result is inscribedGrading is completed and the eligible credential is added to the candidate record.
03

Duties of the candidate

The candidate must complete verification, protect credentials, use compatible technology, preserve authorised downloads and undertake the evaluation personally. A missing stage should be reported without delay.

04

Protected releases and security holds

Materials may be supplied through time-limited or single-use access. Activation creates a delivery record, but a genuine Academy-side failure remains reviewable. Suspected account division, scraping, fraud or identity manipulation may pause the sequence.

05

Interruption and completion

Maintenance, risk review or a security incident may temporarily alter the order of stages. Where a material Academy-side fault prevents access, the appropriate response may be restoration, an extension, repeated delivery or another proportionate remedy.

Fulfilment is complete when the purchased curriculum and access period have been made available and the candidate has received the contracted evaluation opportunity. Award eligibility remains subject to identity, submission and result requirements.

06

Office of inquiry

Report delivery concerns through the Academy contact registry or by telephone at +91 7969654626, +91 8062386662 or +91 2241507377. Identity documents should be supplied only through an authorised secure channel.
