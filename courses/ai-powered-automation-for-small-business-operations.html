Curriculum Overview & Foundations

Small businesses frequently lose hundreds of productive hours each month to repetitive, manual administrative tasks such as sorting emails, manually copy-pasting customer information across independent software systems, updating inventory trackers, and handling baseline customer inquiries. This course is engineered to empower small business operators to design, deploy, and monitor complex AI-driven automated workflows using visual, low-code automation tools. Through self-contained recorded screen-shares and step-by-step PDF logic books, students will learn how to transition standalone AI models into active, automated agents that link disparate business tools together. By removing the need for an expensive in-house software development team or live consultants, this training enables independent businesses to cut operational administrative costs by up to 40% while dramatically accelerating transaction fulfillment times.

The course focuses heavily on logical workflow design, understanding how software applications transfer data via webhooks, and utilizing Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) through visual canvas spaces. Students will learn to map operational bottlenecks, isolate repetitive data transfer points, and embed text-processing AI layers directly into automated routines. Competencies include building automated lead-capture systems, designing smart data routing pathways based on incoming customer sentiment, connecting digital spreadsheets to cloud platforms, and automating the extraction of data from incoming email attachments such as vendor invoices or receipts.
