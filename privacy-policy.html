Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Revised for 2026. Governing the collection, storage, and protection of personal and student data at ELITE TOOLISTIC (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you enroll in courses, register for examinations, request information, or contact our support team. This information may include your name, email address, phone number, billing address, professional background, and identification details necessary for candidate certification.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is utilized to process candidate admissions, deliver curriculum materials, administer examinations, issue accredited certificates, provide customer support, and communicate essential administrative notices regarding course schedules.

3. Data Security & Protection

We implement industry-standard encryption, strict access controls, and secure server configurations to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of student personal information.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with accredited examination partners, statutory authorities where legally mandated, and secure payment processing services strictly required to fulfill transactions.

5. Your Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records held in our registry. For questions or requests regarding your data, please contact our privacy desk at support@elitetoolistic.com.
