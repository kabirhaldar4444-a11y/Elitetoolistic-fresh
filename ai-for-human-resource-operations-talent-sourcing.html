Curriculum Overview & Foundations

Human Resource managers and talent recruiters are heavily weighed down by data-heavy administrative processes, including reading hundreds of resumes per job opening, writing custom role manuals, designing employee onboarding paths, and documenting performance scoring. This 15-day course provides HR specialists and corporate administrators with the practical skills needed to use independent artificial intelligence software to automate internal employee management operations. By following pre-recorded software screenshares and reading comprehensive PDF operational checklists, students will learn to configure custom resume evaluation screens, automate interview question trees, and draft precise employee handbook sections independently, requiring no corporate HR board licensing or external institutional dependencies.

Participants will gain technical proficiency in building text extraction screens to evaluate multiple job applications simultaneously, mapping candidate qualification rankings against targeted job metrics, and writing automated policy guidelines. The downloadable PDF textbooks cover foundational data-anonymization workflows to protect applicant privacy, structured criteria patterns to spot role suitability without human bias, and formatting prompts for employee communications. Competencies include using AI text assistance to structure comprehensive KPI appraisal frameworks, generate customized internal training schedules, and compile employee feedback surveys into clean operational metrics dashboards.
